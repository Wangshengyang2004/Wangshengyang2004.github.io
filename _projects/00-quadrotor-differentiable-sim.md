## Overview
Vision-conditioned quadrotor policies trained in VisFly with full-dynamics differentiable simulation. Policies output collective thrust and body-rate commands to navigate cluttered environments, with ROS-based sim-to-real deployment on physical UAVs (ViSYS Lab, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Jun 2025 – May 2026).
