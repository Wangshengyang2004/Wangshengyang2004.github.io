## Overview
LLM-guided reward shaping and evolution for reinforcement learning of agile quadrotor maneuvers, including double flips, trained in vectorized Isaac Gym, Isaac Sim, and Isaac Lab environments. Reached a 70% improvement over human-designed reward baselines in simulation and zero-shot hover transfer to a physical UAV (Edge Intelligence Lab, Duke Kunshan University).
